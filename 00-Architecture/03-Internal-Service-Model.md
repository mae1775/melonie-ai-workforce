# Internal Workforce Service Model v0.4

## Ownership
All 25 AI agents belong to Melonie Araneta's internal organization. They assist on Melonie-owned brands, daughters' business projects, and third-party client deliverables under Melonie's direction. This is **not** a SaaS platform or a client-facing tenant product.

## Assignment model
`organization_owner = melonie`; `workspace_id` identifies an internal business or project context; `client_id` is optional; `project_id` identifies a scoped engagement; `engagement_owner` identifies the accountable human. All agents remain centrally managed.

## Isolation
Project and client documents are segregated by access policy and purpose. Reuse skills and templates, never confidential client facts, credentials, or unlicensed assets. Access must be validated in deterministic n8n steps before model context is assembled.

## Example
ConvertPrime delivers a client website: Lucas -> Arman (digital) -> Elle/Raf/Bea, with Caleb tracking milestones and Owen validating outputs. Melonie approves client-facing communications and production changes.

## Onboarding
For each engagement: record client/business, scope, permitted data, tool permissions, deadlines, deliverables, acceptance criteria, confidentiality rules, retention and approval owner. Close and archive when complete.
